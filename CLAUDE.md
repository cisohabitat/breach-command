# Breach Command

Project instructions, invariants and the record of why the game is the way
it is live in `AGENTS.md`. Read it first.

The improvement roadmap towards AAA is `docs/ROADMAP.md`: eight phases, each
with exit criteria and the instrument that measures them. Before proposing
visual, content, onboarding or infrastructure work, find the phase it
belongs to and check its criteria; when a phase's criteria are met, record
the date, the commit and the numbers under its heading there.
