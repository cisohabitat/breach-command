# Information density and learning load

0.10.2 keeps the failed-check evidence limit and actual meter changes visible,
with the original failure narrative and pressure explanation under roll details.
Successful finding narratives, voice captions and Listen remain visible. Uncleared
cases lead with completing the chain and response; mastery follows afterward.
The accuracy challenge permits revisions, and retired no-revision awards retain
their original stored ID. Completed-response verdicts name the final service
condition and recovery risk separately from the unchanged score and grade.

0.10.0 keeps practice on its own route, entered from a relevant debrief suggestion
or Settings. Each exercise shows one situation and one call, then its bounded
feedback; the learning record follows in a closed fold rather than competing with the question.
Carry-forward decisions remain folded behind one summary. Optional confidence
reflection is off by default and folded within response decisions, with an explicit
Skip option and no grade penalty. Review uses the public snapshot at commitment,
not hindsight. Practice and its notebook introduce no campaign scoring or mandatory
lesson gate. No measured human learning benefit is claimed.

0.9.64 adds a nonblocking Resume fold: its summary states the next pending
investigation task, with saved check counts and the last result inside. Pending
decisions open directly with their existing context and no extra recap. It disappears
after the first game-state change. Fast resolution is adjustable during play
without skipping important reports or changing turn costs. The sector readout
switches to the published case constraints after two confirmed stages. These
changes add no inference from hidden answers and claim no measured learning gain.

The goal is to help a player explain their next choice and its cost without
reading the full reference material. These are design hypotheses, not measured
human-playtest outcomes. Preserve the engine, exact costs, public-information
boundaries, accessible controls, voice captions and existing saves.

## Follow-up measurement

19 October 2026 refinement: explicit voice status and a Settings sample make
silence understandable; benefit/cost labels and visible uncertainty make
choices comparable. The review leads with one record-based lesson and up to
three calls to revisit. Optional opening plan controls use a native disclosure
without changing costs or choices; keyboard focus stays inside it when a player
returns to defaults. These are presentation changes, with no new human-results
claim. Preserve the existing review details and manual access to every plan.

An automated fresh-profile review on 16 October 2026 checked the opening on a
375 px phone and 1280 px desktop. The academy's first navigation action was
visible without scrolling on both. One wording defect was confirmed before
any check ran: the tutorial requested a source that "supports your hypothesis"
and own-source accessible names announced "supports current hypothesis".
Those descriptions now distinguish a source's ability to test a reading from
the result's support for it. A fresh-profile browser regression follows the
opening through the first procedure and its recorded turn effects, retaining
the academy and shipped guidance defaults. This is an automated review and
regression check, not evidence of newcomer comprehension or human timing.

During the public beta, record whether players can name the current question,
explain signal versus confidence, find every response and describe their

results to decide which additional command, sector or response explanations
